Visor stream, a gaming video streaming and community platform. It will allow gamers to upload, stream, and monetize their content while followers subscribe for exclusive access. With affordable subscription tiers payable via mobile money (M Pesa, MTN, Airtel), Visor addresses the gap left by Twitch and YouTube, which lack localized pricing and payment integration. The long term vision is to scale globally, competing with established platforms by offering esports events, gamification, and community driven features.
